    Instructions & Help

    How to start a test

    1. Choose a difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard) and a duration (30, 60, or 120 seconds).
    2. Click Start Typing Test, then click inside the text box.
    3. Start typing — the timer begins on your first keystroke.
    4. The test ends when time runs out or you finish the whole passage.

    How scores are calculated

    • WPM (words per minute) uses the standard 5-characters-per-word rule:
      WPM = (Correct Characters ÷ 5) ÷ Elapsed Minutes
    • Accuracy = (Correct Characters ÷ Total Characters Typed) × 100. Empty input counts as 100% until you type.
    • Scores are computed from your final text, so deleting and retyping cannot inflate your score — only characters correct at the end count.

    Difficulty levels

    • Easy: short, simple everyday sentences.
    • Medium: longer sentences with commas and mixed words.
    • Hard: advanced vocabulary and punctuation.

    Reading your history

    Each saved attempt shows date, WPM, accuracy, correct/incorrect counts, difficulty, and duration. The line chart plots recent WPM scores so you can compare attempts and watch your average improve.
